CLOVES-4603: Benchmarking Classical Texture Features and Fine-Tuned Deep Models for Clove Quality Grading

Innocent Nyalala 1,2 , Patrick Vincent Ndowo 1
1School of Engineering and Science, Indian Institute of Technology Madras Zanzibar, Tanzania · 2Wadhwani School of Data Science and AI (WSDSAI), Indian Institute of Technology Madras, Chennai, India
CVPR 2026 — 7th Workshop on Vision for Agriculture (CV4A / V4A), Denver, USA (May 2026)
Paper Code Dataset / Split BibTeX

TL;DR: CLOVES-4603 benchmark graphic for the 7th Workshop on Vision for Agriculture at CVPR 2026: 4,603 standardized ZSTC clove images achieving 99.67% deep learning accuracy and 92.94% classical texture-fusion SVM accuracy.

Abstract

We introduce CLOVES-4603, the first public benchmark dataset for automated clove quality grading, comprising 4,603 standardized 224x224 RGB images spanning four commercial grades collected at the Zanzibar State Trading Corporation (ZSTC). We benchmark classical texture pipelines (HOG+SVM, GLCM+GLRLM+LBP fusion) against fine-tuned deep learning models including ResNet-50, MobileNetV3, and a frozen DINOv2 vision transformer. ResNet-50 achieves 99.67% accuracy on the test set, whereas texture-fusion SVM reaches 92.94%, providing a strong and deployment-friendly classical baseline. This benchmark informs real-world deployment trade-offs between accuracy, latency, and computational cost in agricultural settings where infrastructure is limited.
