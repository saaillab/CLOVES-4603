CLOVES-4603: Benchmarking Classical Texture Features and Fine-Tuned Deep Models for Clove Quality Grading

Innocent Nyalala 1,2 , Patrick Vincent Ndowo 1
1School of Engineering and Science, Indian Institute of Technology Madras Zanzibar, Tanzania
2Wadhwani School of Data Science and AI, Indian Institute of Technology Madras, Chennai, India
CVPR 2026: 7th Workshop on Vision for Agriculture (CV4A / V4A), Denver, USA
Paper Code

Zanzibar produces roughly 8,000 tons of dried cloves annually, yet post-harvest commercial grading at official auction depots still relies on subjective visual inspection. CLOVES-4603 introduces an open benchmark of 4,603 standardized 224x224 RGB single-clove images spanning four official Zanzibar State Trading Corporation (ZSTC) commercial grades. Using lot-aware stratified 70/10/20 partitions across five random seeds with bootstrap confidence intervals, we compare classical handcrafted texture pipelines (HOG+SVM and fused GLCM+GLRLM+LBP with RBF-SVM), a frozen self-supervised DINOv2 linear probe, and fine-tuned ImageNet backbones alongside latency and parameter measurements.

Abstract

Zanzibar produces approximately 8,000 tons of dried cloves annually; however, post-harvest quality grading remains manual, inconsistent, and costly. We introduce CLOVES-4603, a dataset of 4,603 standardized 224x224 RGB images spanning four commercial grades collected at the Zanzibar State Trading Corporation facilities. Using lot-aware stratified 70/10/20 splits (5 seeds, bootstrap 95% CIs), we benchmarked classical texture pipelines (HOG+SVM and GLCM+GLRLM+LBP fusion with RBF-SVM), a frozen DINOv2 ViT-B/14 linear probe, and four fine-tuned ImageNet models. On the 921-image test set, ResNet-50 achieved 99.67% accuracy (macro F1 99.66%), and MobileNetV3-Large achieved an accuracy of 99.35%. The frozen DINOv2 probe achieved 94.90%, outperforming the texture fusion SVM at 92.94%. HOG+SVM reaches 32.68%. Accuracy, latency, and parameter trade-offs are provided for deployment. This benchmark establishes a reproducible evaluation standard for whole dried-spice grading research.

Results

On the 921-image held-out test set, fine-tuned ResNet-50 reaches 99.67% accuracy (99.66% macro-F1), whereas mobile-ready MobileNetV3-Large reaches 99.35% accuracy at sub-5 ms GPU inference latency. Without GPU fine-tuning, frozen DINOv2 ViT-B/14 features achieve 94.90% accuracy and CPU-only GLCM+GLRLM+LBP texture fusion with RBF-SVM achieves 92.94%, outperforming shape-gradient HOG+SVM (32.68%) by +60.26 percentage points.
